Sound map: where each letter is made

Five consonant groups move from the throat to the lips. Say each row slowly with the tongue touching the named place.

Full-mouth drill (2 min)

Say each row 5 times, then the whole chart once in one breath-length rhythm. Keep the jaw loose and feel the sound shift forward.
